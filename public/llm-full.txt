## Detailed Service Descriptions

### Reiki Healing
Traditional Usui Reiki channelling universal life-force energy through
the practitioner's hands to remove energetic blockages, reduce stress,
and accelerate natural healing. Sessions range from 30 to 90 minutes.

### Chakra Alignment & Balancing
A diagnostic and therapeutic session targeting the seven primary chakras
using a combination of energy scanning, crystal placement, mantra
chanting, and guided visualization to restore energetic equilibrium.

### Sound Healing
Therapeutic sessions using Tibetan singing bowls, crystal bowls, gongs,
and tuning forks. Sound vibrations interact with the body's energy
field to promote deep relaxation and cellular restoration.

### Crystal Therapy
Curated crystal layouts placed on and around the body to channel,
absorb, and redirect energy flow. Crystals are selected based on the
client's specific energetic needs.

### Pranayama & Breath-work
Structured breathing techniques drawn from yogic tradition. Includes
Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing), Kapalabhati (skull-shining),
Bhramari (bee breath), and progressive deep-breathing sequences.

### Guided Meditation
Practitioner-led meditation sessions covering techniques such as
Yoga Nidra, body scanning, loving-kindness (Metta), and Vedic mantra
meditation for stress management and spiritual growth.

### Yoga & Mindfulness Workshops
Group and private sessions combining Hatha yoga, Vinyasa flow, and
mindfulness practices. Workshops focus on building a sustainable
daily practice for long-term wellbeing.
